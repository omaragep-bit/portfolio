SensorsEmbedded systems

AiRA Wearable Monitoring Concept

AiRA is a sensor-based prototype exploring physiological and movement data. The current concept uses an ESP32 Super Mini for processing and Bluetooth connectivity, a MAX30102 optical sensor for heart-rate and SpO₂ readings, and an MPU6050 IMU for movement and activity tracking.

These signals can support trend monitoring and experimental wellness insights. Any respiratory-rate or sleep-quality values would be estimates, not direct clinical measurements, and the prototype is not a medical diagnostic device.

Components: ESP32 Super Mini · MAX30102 · MPU6050 · breadboard wiring

AiRA — Wearable Asthma Monitoring Concept

A wrist-worn concept for monitoring health-related trends and exploring early-warning support for people with asthma. Planned sensing includes MAX30102 for heart rate and SpO₂ trends, MPU6050 for movement and activity, and an ESP32 Super Mini for processing and Bluetooth communication with a companion app.

Additional options discussed include a skin-temperature sensor and vibration alerts. These are proposed additions, not confirmed as installed. AiRA is a developing concept and is not a substitute for medical assessment or a validated asthma monitor.

Focus: wearable electronics · physiological sensors · movement tracking · Bluetooth app concept
